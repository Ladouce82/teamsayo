From decision to done — in three moments

Most teams don't lack meetings or strategy. They lose track between a decision and its delivery. TeamSayo closes that gap.

01 · CAPTURE

Decisions become tracked actions

Paste notes or import a Teams or Zoom transcript. TeamSayo proposes decisions and action points; a person confirms. Owners, deadlines, reminders and escalation follow automatically.

02 · PROVE

Reports you can stand behind

Draft management, board, client or donor reports in minutes. Every figure links to the record that proves it, and nothing is shared until someone approves it.

03 · SEE

Every company, one view

Run several companies or offices in one workspace. See delivery, capacity, development and achievements side by side — or one company at a time.
